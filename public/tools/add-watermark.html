Add a watermark

Your text over the photo, at the opacity and corner you choose.

Add your own text label to images you are entitled to publish. The watermark is drawn into the exported image and is separate from the original file.

How to use this tool

  1. Add one or more photos and enter your watermark text.
  2. Choose placement, opacity and size; inspect the preview.
  3. Apply the label to the batch and download the resulting images.

Worked example

A short studio name placed in a corner at moderate opacity identifies a photo while leaving the main subject readable. Check both light and dark photos before applying to all.

Limitations and checks

A watermark does not establish copyright or stop copying. The tool exports JPEG, so transparency and animation are not preserved. It adds text rather than a separate logo image.
