Word count

Words, characters and how long it takes to read.

Count words, characters and recurring words in a draft. Estimated reading time is useful for planning a talk or article, while the exact counting convention may differ from an upload portal.

How to use this tool

  1. Paste your text.
  2. Review the word, character and reading-time figures.
  3. Edit the draft and use the destination portal count if it enforces a limit.

Words you repeat most

Type something to see them.

Worked example

A 600-word article takes around three minutes at a 200-word-per-minute assumption. A technical article or spoken presentation may need more time.

Limitations and checks

Reading time is an estimate, not a measurement. Hyphens, punctuation and languages without spaces can lead to different word counts in different products.
