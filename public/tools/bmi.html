Body mass index

BMI, the healthy range, and what it actually tells you.

Calculate adult body mass index from weight and height as a general screening measure. BMI describes a weight-to-height ratio and cannot determine an individual health status.

How to use this tool

  1. Enter weight in kilograms and height in centimeters.
  2. Read the BMI value and adult category.
  3. Discuss health concerns with a qualified clinician rather than changing treatment based on this number.
—
BMICategory (WHO)
< 18.5Underweight
18.5 – 24.9Healthy weight
25 – 29.9Overweight
30 – 34.9Obesity class I
≥ 35Obesity class II or III

Worked example

At 70 kg and 170 cm, BMI = 70 ÷ 1.70² ≈ 24.2. The same BMI can describe people with very different muscle mass and health needs.

Limitations and checks

Adult ranges do not apply to children. Pregnancy, muscle mass, age and other factors affect interpretation. This calculator is educational and does not diagnose disease. See the WHO obesity fact sheet.

Source: WHO: obesity and overweight.

What it is good for, and what it is not

BMI is a population indicator, not a diagnosis. It divides weight by height squared and cannot tell muscle from fat, so athletes frequently land in "overweight", while an older person with little muscle can read "healthy" while carrying too much fat.

Waist measurement is the usual companion check: above 94 cm for men and 80 cm for women there is cardiovascular risk even when BMI looks fine.
