Extract the audio

Pull the sound out of a video as a WAV file.

Decode the soundtrack of a local video and export the selected section as WAV. This is useful for editing sound in another application without keeping the picture.

How to use this tool

  1. Select a video that contains a supported audio track.
  2. Set the section and choose mono or stereo as appropriate.
  3. Extract, listen to the WAV, and save it.

Output is lossless WAV, which every player understands. There is no MP3 option because the browser ships no MP3 encoder and adding one would mean pulling in an external library.

Worked example

For spoken notes, mono can reduce size. Music with left/right spatial information is better kept in stereo. WAV files are commonly larger than compressed audio tracks.

Limitations and checks

WAV export does not create an MP3. Some video audio codecs cannot be decoded by the browser. Extracted audio cannot be cleaner than its source.
