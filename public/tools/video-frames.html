Extract frames

Turn the video into still images and grab them as a ZIP.

Capture still images at intervals within a selected section of a video. This helps create thumbnails or document a sequence without manually pausing at every moment.

How to use this tool

  1. Load the clip and choose start and end times.
  2. Choose the number of frames (1 to 120) and output image format.
  3. Extract the frames, review their timestamps, and save one image or the ZIP.

Worked example

For a ten-second range, choosing six frames samples roughly at 0, 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10 seconds. Choose one frame to capture the midpoint, or use Grab the current frame for a specific preview position.

Limitations and checks

These are decoded browser frames, not guaranteed frame-accurate source samples. Large batches use memory. Smaller source videos cannot produce sharper stills by exporting at a larger size.
