Editorial policy

ToolNez help is written around the tool’s actual input controls, export behavior and limitations. Examples use synthetic values and explain the assumptions required to interpret a result.

Accuracy and scope

We distinguish estimated output size from measured size, local processing from external library downloads, and browser-dependent support from universal compatibility. A tool that reads a PDF text layer is not described as OCR. A re-encoded video is not described as a lossless cut.

Sources and corrections

Implementation details can be checked in the public repository. Medical screening information links to a recognized health source and does not imply that ToolNez has a medical reviewer. Financial calculators disclose rate assumptions and excluded costs. Contact us with an explanation and a reproducible sample if a guide or result is wrong.

Changes and advertising

The change log records substantive site changes. Publication dates describe the page revision; they are not evidence of independent testing or professional review. Ads must not appear on editors, results, contact forms, category navigation, legal notices or error pages.

We do not manufacture user counts, testimonials or professional credentials. Advertising approval is a decision made by Google and is not guaranteed by a minimum word count or number of pages.
